3. HOPE ("There is a way through")

   - Reference exact timing windows and favorable elements.
   - Example: "July through September {{CURRENT_YEAR}} brings Fire to the foreground. For this chart, that is not pressure — it is the visibility you have been waiting for."

4. ACTION ("Here is what to do")
   - Give 3-4 concrete, specific actions the person can take.
   - Each action must be tied to a specific element, month, or chart feature.
   - Bad: "Stay balanced and be mindful."
