The Dog is the guardian of the Chinese zodiac — loyal, protective, and possessed of a moral compass that never wavers. Dogs are the ones you want in your corner when things get hard. They show up. They tell the truth. They fight for what's right even when it costs them. Their loyalty is legendary, their integrity is absolute, and their love — once earned — is unconditional.
