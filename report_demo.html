The Wealth Pattern

Shi Shen (Food God) leads the ten-god structure, with Zheng Guan (Direct Officer) close behind. This is one of the clearest signatures of productive talent combined with a structural need. Value is made through output, service, ideas, teaching, strategy, or other forms of useful creation. But productivity and preservation are not the same thing. Preservation requires a different kind of work — procedural, boring in the best sense, and deeply disciplined.

The guidance therefore places Earth as the primary use god and Metal as the primary support god. Earth slows things down, gives value a place to rest, and builds the habits that make retention automatic. Metal adds the cutting function: sorting, accounting, precision, review, and the willingness to decline what does not fit.

What Helps

  • Budgeting systems, savings rules, and clean account separation with visible tracking.
  • Longer decision windows before large purchases or new financial commitments.
  • Income paths that reward consistency, quality, and repeatable delivery over novelty.
  • Practical mentors, accountants, or operators who bring structure to the work.

What Drains

  • Too many simultaneous projects — movement mistaken for progress.
  • Loose boundaries around lending, sharing, or investing attention without clear terms.
  • Letting emotional momentum make financial decisions that belong to procedure.
  • The belief that talent alone will produce order without routine.

For this chart, the most fitting symbolic direction is wealth retention, not wealth pursuit. The object should support the discipline that holds — not the instinct that chases.

Practical directive: treat money like a field to be cultivated, not a current to be caught. Slower decisions, clearer records, and better filters will outperform any burst of inspiration.
