What this is — and what it isn't

Chinese metaphysics is a cultural framework, not a crystal ball. Here is how we approach it.

What it is

  • A centuries-old way of describing patterns — your element, your chart, your timing.
  • A tool for self-reflection: how you naturally move, what drains you, what to notice.
  • Rooted in the Longhu Mountain (龍虎山) tradition, explained in plain English.

What it isn't

  • Not a prediction of your future, and not a guarantee of any outcome.
  • Not medical, legal, or financial advice.
  • Not a replacement for your own judgment — you stay the decision-maker.
