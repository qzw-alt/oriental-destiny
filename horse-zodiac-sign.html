Horse Personality and Character Traits

The Horse is the free spirit of the Chinese zodiac — energetic, passionate, and driven by an almost physical need for movement, freedom, and self-expression. Horses can't be caged. They bring warmth, enthusiasm, and an infectious energy to every room they enter. They are the adventurers, the explorers, the ones who remind the rest of us that life is meant to be lived, not just managed.
