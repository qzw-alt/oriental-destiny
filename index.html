Frequently Asked Questions

Is this really done by a person?

Yes. Every report is hand-calculated and hand-written by our reading team, trained in the classical Longhu Mountain tradition. We do not use AI to mass-produce answers. The 3–6 hour delivery window exists because your reading is being prepared individually.

What if I don't know my exact birth time?

We can still generate a highly accurate day-based reading. The Hour Pillar (which influences later-life themes) will be omitted, and we will note this in your report.

Why does it take 3–6 hours?

A genuine BaZi reading requires calculating True Solar Time, plotting the Four Pillars, analyzing the Ten Gods and elemental balance, checking your current Luck Pillar, and then writing a coherent, personalized narrative. This cannot be rushed without losing depth.

Can I ask follow-up questions?

Follow-up questions are included with the Premium Consultation. For the Complete Destiny Book, you may purchase a follow-up session separately.
