The Love Pattern

Direct Resource leads the ten-god structure, with Friend close behind. This is the signature of a person who gives support naturally, who learns through relationship, and who tends to become what others need. But giving and receiving are not the same motion. The chart shows a giver who sometimes forgets to ask. A supporter who rarely names her own needs. A person whose love is deep but whose boundaries are thin.

The guidance therefore places Earth as the primary use god and Fire as the primary support god. Earth contains the emotional flood, gives feeling a place to rest, and builds the steadiness that makes love sustainable rather than exhausting. Fire transforms Water into warmth — the kind of warmth that radiates without depleting, that gives without losing the self.

  • Partners who value steadiness over drama — people who show up consistently, not just intensely.
  • Time alone to replenish — Water cannot pour indefinitely without a source.

  • Partners who take without reciprocating — emotional vampires who mistake her depth for an infinite well.
  • Relationships that live in crisis — intensity mistaken for intimacy, turbulence for passion.
  • The habit of adjusting herself to keep peace — losing shape to maintain connection.
  • The belief that love means self-sacrifice — that boundaries are unkind.
