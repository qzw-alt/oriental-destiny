Frequently Asked Questions

Is this really done by a real person?

Yes. Every report is calculated and written by one of our ordained Taoist masters. We do not use AI to generate the interpretations. The 3–6 hour delivery window exists because your master is working on your chart by hand.

What is Longhu Mountain?

Longhu Mountain (龙虎山) in Jiangxi Province is the birthplace of Taoism and one of China's most sacred spiritual mountains. It has been the center of Taoist talismanic tradition for over 1,900 years. Our masters are ordained within this lineage.

What if I don't know my exact birth time?

We can still perform a highly accurate day-based reading. However, the Hour Pillar (which influences later-life themes and children) will be omitted. We will note this in your report.

Why does it take 3–6 hours?

A genuine BaZi reading cannot be rushed. Your master must calculate your True Solar Time, plot the Four Pillars, analyze the Ten Gods and elemental balance, check your current Luck Pillar, and then write a coherent, personalized narrative. This takes time.

Can I ask follow-up questions?

Follow-up questions are included with the Premium Consultation. For the Complete Destiny Book, you may purchase a follow-up session separately if you need clarification.
