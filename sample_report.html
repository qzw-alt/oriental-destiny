Vol.4 In Practice In Practice
  • 1 Green is your switch. Tomorrow morning, before you check your phone, put on one green thing — socks, a bracelet, even just green underwear nobody sees but you. This isn't superstition. It's a signal to your subconscious that today, you're choosing growth over stability.
  • 2 Once a week, take a walk in a park or any place with trees — no phone, no podcast, just you and the green. Notice how the trees don't rush. They just grow, slowly, in their own time. You're allowed to do the same.
  • 3 Every evening, write down one thing you did for yourself that day — even if it's just 'drank water without rushing.' This is training your brain to see yourself as someone worth investing in, not just someone who invests in others.
  • 4 When you feel the urge to say 'yes' to something you don't want to do, pause and ask: 'If I say no, what's the worst that happens?' Then say no. Out loud. It'll feel like breaking a bone, but it's just your Wood finally pushing through the concrete.
