# Oriental Destiny Release Checklist

## 1. Domain and site access

- Confirm the live domain opens the home page correctly.
- Confirm mobile and desktop both load without broken layout.
- Confirm these pages open normally:
  - `index.html`
  - `instant_reading.html`
  - `products.html`
  - `checkout.html`
  - `thanks.html`

## 2. Form capture

- Submit one real test entry through the checkout form.
- Confirm Formspree receives:
  - email
  - contact method
  - birth date
  - birth time
  - birth location
  - gender
  - notes
  - selected plan
  - selected carrier
- Confirm the saved submission is easy for you to read and follow up on.

## 3. PayPal payment

- Run one real payment test with the hosted PayPal button.
- Confirm the payment amount is exactly `USD 99.00`.
- Confirm the PayPal product title and description look clean to the buyer.
- Confirm payment works on both PayPal balance and card flow if available.

## 4. End-to-end order flow

- Fill the instant reading page first.
- Click through to checkout.
- Save order details.
- Complete PayPal payment.
- Confirm you can match the payment with the submitted customer details.

## 5. Manual fulfillment readiness

- Prepare one simple follow-up email template for new buyers.
- Prepare one confirmation message for:
  - recommended material
  - bracelet or pendant choice
  - final symbolic direction
- Decide where you will track orders:
  - email only
  - spreadsheet
  - Notion
  - CRM

## 6. Trust and policy basics

- Make sure contact email is active and checked daily.
- Make sure your wording stays consistent:
  - physical jewelry
  - personalized matching
  - final item confirmed before shipping
- Keep disclaimer wording visible on key pages.

## 7. Launch decision

You are ready to publish when:

- the form definitely captures customer details
- PayPal definitely accepts payment
- you can manually match one payment to one order
- you are ready to reply to buyers within 24 hours
