The Rooster in the Chinese Zodiac — Origin, Mythology & Position

The Rooster (Ji 鸡, branch You 酉) is the tenth animal of the twelve-year zodiac cycle and the only bird among its companions who is granted a top-ten position. Its place at number ten is more than a matter of counting — it marks the Rooster as the keeper of the evening watch, the one whose crow announces both dawn and dusk. In the banquet race that, according to the popular myth recorded in folk almanacs and village oral tradition, determined the zodiac order, the Rooster's crowing is what guided the animals across the river, making the Rooster the calendar's punctual witness.

From a BaZi perspective, the Rooster belongs to the Yin Metal element (You-Metal 酉金). Where Yang Metal (Geng 庚) is the raw ore, the great sword, the public edifice, Yin Metal is the polished surface — the mirror, the jewel, the fine needle, the surgical instrument, the bell. The Rooster's Metal has been smelted, hammered, cooled and refined. It is the metal that has surrendered its brute force in exchange for precision and beauty. This is the technical, aesthetic and moral signature of every Rooster-native chart.

The Rooster sits in the West (Xi 西) and governs early autumn — the season when the leaves begin to dry and the air turns crisp. Early autumn is the moment when summer's lush abundance is judged and pruned. Anything that cannot survive the cool, dry clarity of early autumn is shed. This is why Rooster-native people have a reputation for decisive editing — of work, of relationships, of projects, of self. The Rooster cuts without cruelty, but it does cut.

The Rooster is also one of the four cardinal pillars of the Metal trigram group, sitting opposite the Rabbit (Mao 卯) in the six-clash cycle and harmonising with the Ox (Chou 丑) and the Snake (Si 巳) in the San He (Three Harmony) Metal frame. Anything you learn about the Rooster is in dialogue with these three companions — the deep earth that holds the metal, the hidden fire that forges it, and the gentle wood that the metal shapes.

The Rooster's Day Master — Personality and Core Nature

The zodiac sign tells us the branch — the social mask. The Day Master (Ri Yuan 日元) tells us the soul. The Day Master is the heavenly stem on the day you were born and is the single most important data point in any BaZi chart. It is the lens through which a BaZi reader interprets everything else in your Four Pillars: how wealth treats you, how your relationships feel, which careers feel meaningful, which years are dangerous and which are golden.

You can find your Day Master by reading our What Is the Day Master in BaZi primer or by submitting your birth details on the free instant reading page. For a Rooster-born person, the most resonant Day Masters and their flavour are:

Metal Day Masters (Geng and Xin) — The Pure Rooster

Xin-Metal (Yin) is the most common Day Master for Rooster-born people because the cycle naturally aligns. Xin is the polished needle; combined with You-Metal branch, it produces a personality of unusual precision and aesthetic refinement. Geng-Metal (Yang) Day Masters are also excellent for Rooster-natives because Yang-Metal produces Yin-Metal in the output cycle — Metal flowing into Metal — making the Rooster articulate in technical execution. Read the Xin Day Master and Geng Day Master guides for the full signature.

Earth Day Masters (Wu and Ji) — The Supporter of Metal

Earth produces Metal. A Rooster-born person with a Wu or Ji Day Master is constantly generating their own resource energy. They tend to look grounded, dependable and quietly confident. See the Wu Day Master and Ji Day Master profiles for the deeper analysis.

Water, Wood & Fire Day Masters — When the Rooster's Element Diverges

Less common but deeply significant are Rooster-born people whose Day Master is Ren, Gui, Jia, Yi, Bing or Ding. These charts create a tension between the branch element and the Day Master element — sometimes a clash, sometimes a beautiful harmony. This is why two 1981 "Metal Roosters" can live in completely different emotional worlds.

Rooster Personality Through the Five Elements Lens (BaZi Psychology)

BaZi reading has a much sharper vocabulary than the sun-sign zodiac. The Rooster, when read through the Five Elements, is not a single archetype but a family of personalities distinguished by which element is strongest in the birth chart. Each configuration produces a recognisable inner type:

Metal-dominant Rooster. Disciplined, principled, structured. Metal Roosters have a quiet moral code and a reputation for absolute follow-through. They are the kind of people who, when they say they will deliver on Friday, deliver on Friday.

Earth-dominant Rooster. The anchor. Earth Roosters finish what other Roosters start. They are slower to commit but utterly reliable once they do, and they tend to build the longest marriages, the most durable companies and the deepest friendships.

Fire-dominant Rooster. Passionate, expressive, charismatic. The Fire Rooster is the warmest and most visible of all Rooster variants — performers, presenters, hosts, designers whose work is shown rather than filed.

Water-dominant Rooster. The hidden depth. Water Roosters read people and rooms with the patience of an old counsellor. They speak less than you expect and remember everything they heard.

Wood-dominant Rooster. Generous, ideological, networked. Wood Roosters pull people into projects rather than commanding them. Their empathy is real and quietly visible.

Rooster Strengths and Shadow Sides

No Rooster chart is one note. Even the most gifted Rooster has tendencies that pull against their gifts. Knowing both sides is what allows a Rooster to lead themselves wisely through decades of changing luck.

Core Strengths

  • Sharp observational skills that catch errors before they escalate
  • Exceptional competence — does things properly or not at all
  • Honest, direct, willing to deliver uncomfortable truths kindly
  • Natural talent for organising chaos into functional systems
  • Aesthetic precision — the Rooster's taste is its signature
  • Punctuality, follow-through and the rare ability to finish cleanly

Common Tendencies to Watch

  • Can be overly critical, wounding others with their directness
  • Perfectionism that delays completion and creates unnecessary stress
  • Tendency to be rigid — "my way or the highway" thinking
  • May come across as arrogant or dismissive of others' approaches
  • Struggles to admit when they are wrong
  • Hoards emotional expression and confuses silence with strength

Rooster Compatibility — Love, Marriage, and BaZi Pairings

Rooster compatibility is not luck. It is chemistry — one branch (You) meeting another, modulating through the Ten Gods of each person's Day Master. The most harmonious pairings fall into two groups: the San He (Three Harmony) with Ox and Snake, where the three branches form a complete Metal frame; and the Liu He (Six Harmony) with the Dragon, where Rooster-Dragon (You-Chen) creates a complementary partnership of ideal plus execution.

The most challenging pairings are the Six Clash (Liu Chong) with the Rabbit — direct opposite branch, one Yin one Yin, totally different rhythm — and the Self-Penalty (Zi Xing) with another Rooster, where two perfectionists under one roof halve the grace and double the criticism. The Rooster tends to thrive in roles with visible output and clear standards, and to starve in roles with hidden outputs, vague metrics or politically messy promotion ladders. The Rooster as a Leader

Rooster leaders lead through standards rather than charisma. They tend to build quiet authority, set exacting benchmarks, and outlast louder personalities. Their weakness is occasional rigidity — a perfectionism that becomes a bottleneck at scale. The fix is the same as the chart fix: build a team whose Day Masters complement yours.

Rooster Love and Relationships

In love, the Rooster is one of the zodiac's most quietly devoted partners. They are not the loudest lover, not the most overtly romantic, not the one who floods the relationship with grand gestures — but they are the one who shows up, on time, every single day, with the same precision they bring to everything else. Roosters fall through respect first, affection second, and they commit slowly and almost never reverse the decision.

For marriage, the Rooster is best matched with someone whose Day Master brings warmth (Fire or Earth element), so the Rooster's Metal can be polished without being worn down. Ox + Rooster works because Yin-Earth holds Yin-Metal in a stable container; Snake + Rooster works because the Snake's hidden fire keeps the Rooster's Metal from rusting; Dragon + Rooster works because the Dragon's noble ambition gives the Rooster a horizon worth their exacting standards. Rooster Health — Elemental Balance and Wellbeing

Health in BaZi is read through the Five Elements — which element is in surplus and which is missing. A Rooster-native born in a chart dominated by Earth and Water is typically robust, with strong lungs and skin (Metal), digestion (Earth), and kidneys (Water). A Rooster-native born into a chart with weak Metal or excessive Fire may experience respiratory sensitivity, skin inflammation, or chronic dryness. Rooster health is fundamentally a question of Yin-Metal regulation.

Organ Systems the Rooster Should Protect

The Rooster governs the lungs, large intestine, skin, and respiratory system. Autumn-born Roosters are more sensitive to dry air and seasonal allergens. Excess Fire in the annual or luck pillar (such as 2026's Fire Horse year) adds heat to the system, which can manifest as throat irritation, rashes, or lung inflammation. This is why this Rooster year needs more hydration, lung-supporting herbs, and skin-protective routines than usual.

Lifestyle Recommendations for the Rooster

Prioritise clean air, hydration, oil-rich diets, and gentle lung-tonifying routines. Limit dry, fried and heavily processed foods. Movement practices like swimming, tai chi, qi gong, and slow long-distance running serve the Rooster body better than high-intensity interval training. Rooster Wealth — Money, Investment & Abundance

The Rooster is one of the zodiac's most disciplined money signs. Wealth comes to the Rooster not through single dramatic wins but through patient accumulation, careful research and a refusal to deploy capital without conviction. Roosters read markets, products and contracts better than almost any other sign, which is why they are best-positioned for value investing, fine assets (art, watches, property), quality equities and long-horizon compound wealth rather than leveraged speculation.

Rooster Money Personality

Rooster money personality is conservative by default and aggressive when they trust the craftsmanship. The Rooster will sit on cash patiently until they identify an asset whose quality, design or governance meets their standards — then deploy it decisively. The biggest wealth risk for Roosters is over-saving in growth environments, or — paradoxically — impulsive "feeling-sure" bets driven by Seven Killings energy in their chart.

Best Vehicles for Rooster Wealth

Index funds, real estate, equity in their own businesses, blue-chip equity positions, fine-art and watch collecting, and long-tenor assets that compound quietly. The Rooster should generally avoid leverage-heavy, short-tenor trades and any investment that requires them to out-pace a market they cannot see.
